\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Why a synthetic primary benchmark}

Section~\ref{sec:external} reports the survey in full; the conclusion drives this
section. No public dataset is built for Zero Trust or continuous authorization, and
none delivers user identity together with client TLS fingerprints as given fields. A
corpus that would populate the five-node chain at scale, with labelled lateral movement
and credential reuse, does not exist. We therefore build one, and treat the honesty of
that construction as an object of measurement rather than an assumption.

\subsection{The generator}

The generator simulates the chain \textit{ip} $\rightarrow$ \textit{config (JA3)}
$\rightarrow$ \textit{device} $\rightarrow$ \textit{user} $\rightarrow$ \textit{resource}
over a population of users, devices, sources, client configurations and procedurally
generated resources, with circadian arrival rates, roaming and NAT, shared devices,
cookie wipes, service accounts issuing periodic batch traffic, and benign human error.
It emits binary labels, a per-class type vector and a benign-context bitmask.

One design decision is load-bearing. Benign traffic performs \emph{legitimate
non-habitual accesses} with non-zero probability. Without this, lateral movement would
be feature-identical to ``unusual destination'' and the task would collapse into the
tautology \emph{non-habitual $\Leftrightarrow$ lateral} --- a detector could score
perfectly by learning nothing about time. With it, a lateral event is
indistinguishable from a benign exploratory access on the access edge alone, and the
only discriminator left is the temporal pattern.

We report this because it nearly invalidated our own results. An enrichment of the
generator --- more resources, power-law popularity sampling, byte-volume and status
columns --- introduced severe label leakage in a single change. Benign traffic
concentrated on popular resources while every attack branch sampled uniformly, so a
column carrying the resource index reached AUC \FloorLatPre{} on \emph{every} class by
itself, which is as high as the lateral AUC the full model was reporting. Four
constant-valued patterns identified four populations at 100\% precision and recall.

The remedy was not to soften the claims but to fix the generator: popularity decoupled
from the resource index by a seed-dependent permutation, the resource universe made
seed-dependent, every attack branch drawing its destination from the \emph{same}
sampler as benign traffic, exact-value fingerprints replaced by continuous
distributions, and response-side fields removed as a causality violation.
Table~\ref{tab:floor} shows the effect. Lateral movement fell from \FloorLatPre{} to
\FloorLatPost{}: the task did not merely become more honest, it became genuinely
harder.

A permanent audit now runs on three seeds in seconds on CPU and enforces, on every
generator change: a bound on single-feature AUC with a motivated allowlist; absence of
exact-value fingerprints; equality of the destination marginal between benign and
anomalous traffic (by effect size, not by $p$-value --- at $n \approx 45{,}000$ a
Kolmogorov--Smirnov test rejects on negligible differences); and that every
(route, method) pair emitted is one the route actually serves. \textbf{The two classes
the contribution rests on, lateral movement and credential reuse, have no exemptions},
and a test enforces that they never acquire any.

\subsection{Protocol}

Unless stated otherwise: \PanelAsettings. Training sees benign events only. The test
stream is replayed chronologically, event by event, under the external commit gate of
Section~\ref{sec:problem} --- never under the model's own verdict. Dispersion is
reported as sample standard deviation over seeds with $\text{ddof}=1$; paired
comparisons use a per-seed paired difference with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test and a
bootstrap confidence interval.

\subsection{Baselines}

Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, a static graph neural network (same graph structure,
no temporal memory), and XGBoost. \textbf{All of them receive the same tabular signals
as the TGN} --- the causal history counters and the same precursor prior --- so the gap
isolates the temporal-relational machinery rather than the counters. The static GNN is
the load-bearing comparison: it has the graph and the counters and lacks only the
recurrent memory and temporal neighbourhood. XGBoost is \emph{supervised}: it sees the
labels, it is outside the one-class paradigm, and it is quoted as a reference upper
bound, not as a comparable baseline.

\subsection{A second synthetic stream}

An independently constructed synthetic ZTA stream is being incorporated as a second
evaluation substrate, to test whether the conclusions survive a generator we did not
write --- the sharpest available check on the risk that our results are properties of
our own simulator. Results on it are not included in this draft.
