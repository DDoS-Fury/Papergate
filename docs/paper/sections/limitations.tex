\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

We list these at the level of detail we would want from a paper we were reviewing.

\subsection{Run-to-run variance and multi-seed dispersion}

Two runs of the \emph{same} configuration at the \emph{same} seed, on an identical
stream with identical positive counts, differed by \RunToRunAuc{} in lateral AUC and
\RunToRunRecall{} in lateral recall. While the measured lateral-AUC delta from the
configuration node (\BlatAucD) clearly exceeds this noise floor, the dispersions we report
(\PublishedSdAuc{} on AUC, \PublishedSdRecall{} on recall) come from three seeds at one
run each. Full seeding and deterministic algorithms are in place, and expanding to a
larger grid across additional seeds remains ongoing work.

\subsection{The primary evaluation is synthetic}

With the real-data results withdrawn pending regeneration, the synthetic stream is the
principal exposure of this work. We have tried to make that exposure inspectable rather
than deniable --- the leakage audit of Section~\ref{sec:setup} is permanent, runs on
every generator change, and grants no exemption to the two classes the contribution
rests on. It bounds the ways a generator can make its own task trivial; it cannot
establish that the generator resembles a real enterprise. PicoDomain
(Section~\ref{sec:external}) is a corrective, not a substitute.

\subsection{Operating recall on lateral movement is not solved}

Cost-sensitive routing converts ranking quality into operating recall, but the result
remains a trade-off dial, not a closed problem: recall rises with the benign
false-positive rate, and the deployment chooses the point. We report both endpoints
rather than the flattering one.

\subsection{The bounded-state claim is narrower than it sounds}

Constant-time lookup and bounded memory hold for the \emph{tensor} buffers --- entity
memory, neighbour ring buffer, static features --- which are pre-allocated at fixed
size. They do not hold for the whole system. The interaction counters and alert state
are unbounded dictionaries that grow with every committed pair and are persisted in the
checkpoint. Eviction is not constant-time: selecting a victim scans the capacity, and
invalidating inbound references scans the neighbour buffer. Measured latency is
$P_{50} \approx \LatencyPFifty$\,ms / $P_{99} \approx \LatencyPNinetyNine$\,ms
end-to-end, with five neighbourhood expansions and five encoder passes per request.
This is a known scalability ceiling and the system is presented at that ceiling.

\subsection{The poisoning gate has two residual failure modes}

Both follow from the design rather than from an implementation gap. An adversary whose
events the policy engine admits still enters the baseline. And an entity persistently
scored anomalous is never learned, so a legitimate change in behaviour can starve.
Mitigation is delegated to the orchestrator: the policy engine as the decision
authority, plus a short grace period for newly admitted entities.

\subsection{The precursor is a heuristic}

The kill-chain prior assumes lateral movement follows reconnaissance noisy enough to
trip a sensor on the same origin. It holds in the generator. An adversary who skips the
noisy reconnaissance bypasses it. It is an honest additive prior, not a guarantee.

\subsection{Cold start}

A new entity has no habits to deviate from. In our stream every lateral event lands on
an already-warm entity, so this is not the bottleneck here --- but in deployment a cold
entity is uncovered until it accumulates interactions, and an adversary who knows this
can exploit the window.

\subsection{Fingerprint evasion}

The configuration node detects an \emph{unfamiliar} client, not a malicious one. An
adversary who faithfully clones the victim's TLS stack presents a familiar fingerprint
and defeats the mechanism. The node raises the cost of credential reuse; it does not
eliminate the class.

\subsection{The serving endpoints are unauthenticated}

The commit and score endpoints carry no authentication: anyone who reaches the service
can alter its state and bypass the gate. The design assumes a trusted orchestrator on a
private network. This is a deployment prerequisite, not a residual risk, and we state
it because the anti-poisoning argument of Section~\ref{sec:problem} is void without it.
